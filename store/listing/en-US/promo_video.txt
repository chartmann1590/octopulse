3. Paste into Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing → Promo video (optional) → YouTube URL.
4. Also add same URL to store/listing/en-US/youtube_url.txt and fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/video.txt for automation docs.

YouTube video must be enabled for embedding. Do not set Made for Kids.
