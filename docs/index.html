Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

From the workshop to the couch — keep an eye on every print, nudge the controls, and launch the next job without touching your computer.

🔍

Auto-discovery on Wi-Fi

Finds OctoPrint via mDNS/Bonjour, SSDP and smart subnet scanning. No IP guessing.

  • Works with OctoPi, Docker, custom hosts
  • Filters false positives (real OctoPrint only)
⚡

1-Click Application Keys

Tap “Pair & Connect” → approve in OctoPrint browser → you're in. No copy-paste.

  • Official /api/plugin/appkeys flow
  • Fallback to manual API key if needed
📷

Live camera streaming

Watch prints in real time with snapshot + MJPEG support, flip/rotate and retry logic.

  • Low-latency WebView streaming
  • Auto-reconnect on Wi-Fi bounce
📐

G-code 2D & 3D viewer

Inspect toolpaths layer-by-layer before you print. Pinch-zoom, orbit, slice preview.

  • Parses moves & extrusion accurately
  • Current print quick-load
🎛️

Full printer control

Jog XYZ, home, extrude/retract, set temps & fan, emergency stop — all from your phone.

  • PLA/PETG/ABS/TPU presets
  • Interactive terminal (send any G-code)
🔔

Smart notifications

Get notified on finish, error, or milestones (25/50/75/90%). Per-printer toggles.

  • Background polling + local notifs
  • Configurable intervals

Transparency

Free, with respectful ads.

OctoPulse stays free by showing ads. We keep them lightweight and never interrupt a print.

📢 Why ads?

Developing, testing and hosting OctoPulse costs time and infrastructure. Ads via Google AdMob help keep the app free, open and updated — without selling your data.

  • ▭Banner ads — small, anchored at the bottom. Dismissible and never covering controls.
  • ⧉Interstitial ads — shown only after non-critical actions (e.g., after adding a printer), never during printing.
  • 🛡️No sensitive data collected. Ads use Google's Advertising ID (AD_ID) and basic device info to serve relevant ads. See Privacy Policy.
