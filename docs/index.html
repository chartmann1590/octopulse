Features

Everything you need to print better.

Check on prints from the couch, the kitchen, or the workshop — no computer needed.

🔍

Finds your printer automatically

OctoPulse looks for your printer on your Wi-Fi. No need to type addresses.

⚡

Connects with one tap

Just tap your printer and approve it in OctoPrint. No keys to copy.

📷

Watch your print live

See your print from your phone with a live camera view.

📐

Check before you print

Preview layers in 2D and 3D so you catch issues before they happen.

🎛️

Control everything

Move, heat, cool, and manage prints right from your phone.

🔔

Stay in the know

Get a friendly alert when your print finishes or needs attention.

How it works

From install to printing in a minute.

1

Install and open

Install OctoPulse on your Android phone and connect to the same Wi-Fi as your printer.

→
2

Find and connect

Tap Find Printers, choose yours, and approve it in OctoPrint.

→
3

Print

Watch progress and camera, pause or stop if needed, and start your next print.

Private — stays on your phone and printer No account needed Open source on GitHub →

Questions, answered.

Simple answers about OctoPulse. Still curious? Ask on GitHub or sponsor the project.

We’re finishing the final checks for Google Play. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the day it’s live — or download it early from GitHub Releases.
Yes. OctoPulse is free to download and use. Small ads keep it free — they never pop up while you’re printing.
No. There’s no account, no sign-up. Just open the app and connect to your printer on your Wi-Fi.
If your printer uses OctoPrint — yes. That includes OctoPi on a Raspberry Pi and most setups that run OctoPrint. If you can open OctoPrint in your browser, OctoPulse can connect.
No. Everything stays between your phone and your printer on your Wi-Fi. We don’t see your prints, files, or camera. Privacy Policy
OctoPulse works best when your phone and printer are on the same Wi-Fi. If you’ve set up remote access for OctoPrint, you can enter that address in the app.
You’ll see a small banner at the bottom and sometimes a full-screen ad after you finish a setup step — never during a print. You can control ad personalization in your phone’s settings.
Yes. The code is open on GitHub under the MIT license. You can view it, suggest changes, or build it yourself.
