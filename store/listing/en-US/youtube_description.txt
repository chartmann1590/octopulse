Your OctoPrint in your pocket. Meet OctoPulse — the free, privacy-first OctoPrint companion for Android.

OctoPulse lets you find your printer on Wi-Fi in seconds, pair with one tap, and monitor every print from your phone — live camera, progress, temps, and full control. No account, no cloud.

🔍 Auto-discovery — mDNS/Bonjour + SSDP + smart subnet scan, strict OctoPrint verification
⚡ 1-tap pairing — official Application Keys flow, just tap ALLOW in OctoPrint
📊 Dashboard — multi-printer status cards, progress % & temps
📷 Live camera — MJPEG/snapshot with flip/rotate & auto-reconnect
🎛️ Full control — jog XYZ, home, extrude/retract, fan, temps, emergency stop, terminal
